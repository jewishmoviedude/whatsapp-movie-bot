AI providers — when Gemini cannot answer

        
Gemini's free tier allows 20 requests a day, which is gone by mid-morning; after that every film was judged on its rating alone. The nudity check and the film lookup now ask these providers, in this order, and the first that answers decides. The switches that turn the film lookup and the nudity check on and off are in Movies → Movie data; this card only chooses who answers.

        

          The nudity check only ever helps a film through.
          
It is asked only about a film or episode its rating blocks, and never refuses anything: a “contains nudity” leaves it blocked on its rating, as it would have been anyway. A “no nudity” sends it only from a provider you tick May clear rated films for (and with Also send rated films and episodes with no nudity, in Movies → Movie data, on); a provider without the tick is not asked. Groq, Cerebras, Mistral and OpenRouter run open models that remember particular films less reliably than Gemini.

Discord (read-only) 🎫 …

        
Reads the answers to the support tickets you open with the Jellyfin host in their Discord server, so the hourly check can follow them up: every 30 minutes it reads the new messages in your ticket channels (the ones the Ticket Tool bot makes, ticket-0001 and so on) and keeps them for 90 days. It only ever reads. It cannot post, react, edit, mark anything read or show you typing, and it asks Discord for a handful of things each time, never more.

  • Running a personal Discord account from a program is against Discord's terms. Discord can ban the account for it.

⚠️ Rated-R override — films and TV

        

          This sends a film or an episode the rating would otherwise block.
          
Every title added here is one you are personally vouching for. A rating is
            one letter for the whole film (or TV-MA for a whole episode) — it does not
            say whether it is for violence, language, or nudity — so the bot cannot tell them
            apart and blocks all of them. This list is how you say “not that one”.

          
Everything an entry covers is sent, with no AI nudity check.
            That is the operator's rule (29 September 2026): a title added here is already
            vouched for, so it is not put to the AI check at all — whatever its rating, and
            whatever a model would have said. An entry says send this.

          
A title covers films and TV alike: the film or show named
            exactly that, with case, spaces and punctuation ignored, and nothing
            longer. full metal alchemist covers Fullmetal Alchemist,
            every episode the rating would hold back, and not Fullmetal Alchemist:
            Brotherhood, which needs an entry of its own typed in full; Heat
            covers Heat and not Heat and Dust. Type a name as the
            library has it, in any script. Add a year to narrow it to that year (a name that
            ends in one, like Blade Runner 2049, still covers itself), or
            season 1 / S01E14 to one season or
            episode of a show. Or paste a Jellyfin link for exactly one film, series, season
            or episode. The list shows what an entry matches in the library right now —
            which is what it sends, so read it after adding one.

          
Added titles are recorded in the audit log with your name
            against them. Post something to a group that shouldn't be there and it does not
            come back.

Content check — what is actually in the film

        
A rating conflates violence, language and sex — which is how an R earned on violence was blocked. For a film or episode its rating blocks, this asks what the published content advisories say, and a sure “no nudity” sends it. A film its rating lets through is sent without asking; unrated films are blocked or not as Block films with no rating at all says; when blocked, they are asked about like a rated film.
