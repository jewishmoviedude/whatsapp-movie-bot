*📋 GROUP RULES — please read*

*This is a requests-only group. Absolutely no chatting.*

*HOW TO REQUEST*
• In ONE message, type *#request* followed by the movie's correct official name and the year. *CASE SENSITIVE.*
• *Text only* — no pictures, screenshots, voice notes, or links.
• No tagging, no replying to messages, no "thank you", no yes/no — *only actual requests.*
• *One movie at a time.* Requesting more than one at a time gets your request deleted.
• *We will not do the research for you.*

*REPOSTING*
• During the week you may repost a request *only once every 24 hours.*
• If you see that someone already requested the movie you want, *do not request it again* — you'll be removed.
• *Do not repeat your request over the weekend (Friday–Sunday).*
• Do NOT tag or reply to a previous request (no periods or anything else) — it will be deleted.

*NOT ALLOWED*
• *No rated-R movies.*
• *No movies that are only in theaters — this results in removal.* A 🎪 reaction means the movie is in theaters only and cannot be requested again. Join the "recently released" group to redo that request later.

*WHAT THE REACTIONS MEAN*
• 🎬 — your request is being worked on
• 🎪 — the movie is only in theaters (can't be requested)

*POSTING*
• Movies are posted *only in the Posts group.*
• If you have a movie to post and you aren't an admin on the Posts group, *PM an admin.*
• If you know someone who "actually posts all the time," have them message the admin.

*GOOD TO KNOW*
• Most admins don't post at night or on weekends.
• Nobody is getting paid. Requesting a movie properly doesn't obligate anyone to send it — if your request is fulfilled, great; if not, try again the next day.

*ENFORCEMENT*
• *Two strikes and you're removed.*
• Not following the instructions, or sending the wrong formats, gets you removed or banned. The rules are self-explanatory — please use common sense.
